### Club History

This club was founded on October 13th, 2022 by our former President and Founder Taylor, and since then it has steadily grown into what it is today.

> I transferred to USF in 2022, and I knew basically no one. My first thought was to join clubs bc thats how you can make friends, but The clubs at USF were VERY lackluster in terms of networking if you werent interested in Frats/Sororities. The interest clubs at the time were DEAD as fuck too. I did attend some of the Pride Club events, but those too seemed not geared to making new friends, but rather being a space for a small already established group of friends to hang out and ostracize new people. 
{: .small}


>Because of this, I had enough and wanted to make a club where ANYONE, regardless of any status such as year, age, sexuality, friends etc could instantly join and make friends. Being a furry I obv searched for furry clubs at USF.
{: .small}


> I stumbled upon an unofficial furry club at usf, run by Stasi at the time. I spoke to her and Parker (who was one of the few active members of that discord) and convinced them to help me make a RSO (Registered Student Org) for furries at USF. I was inspired by WOOF (Wisconsin Org Of Furries) in name, and made FLOOF (Florida Org Of Furries)!
{: .small}


> It was very easy to make the club, just took many steps. Parker (who agreed to be treasurer) had to take a short online course for financial handling, I had to take a webinar for starting a club, we both had to find a club sponsor and 5 other people to start as members and boom! we had FLOOF officiated once we wrote and submitted the first official Consitution.
{: .small} 


> For the Faculty Advisor, we chose Dr Mahoney because he had co-authored papers on the furry fandom, and was an anthropologist himself. Besides the obvious irony of being an ANTHROpologist, he genuinely was interested in digital subculture and had studied the furry fandom since 2013! Parker and I met with him and we talked for a few hours about furries and our ideas for the club and he was MORE than happy to be our advisor. We've had him as the advisor ever since and he has repeatedly encouraged his students to join FLOOF!
{: .small}
<cite>— Taylor (Founder)</cite>
{: .small}

> I remember when we first started the club! We were a small dedicated group of mutual friends who were optimistic and excited to make a safe and fun furry space at USF. I remember when I used to be surprised that we were able to fill a room with twenty people, and now FLOOF has events that can host near triple that amount- with full fundraising! It's been a privilege to watch it grow and be a part of this community.
{: .small} 
<cite>— Alexandra (Former Ad Manager & Designer of Stoney)</cite>
{: .small}


> When I first came into USF 2021, I made a couple of servers on the USF Discord Student hub. Eventually, Taylor transferred over, and she proposed the idea of making a furry RSO at USF! As I had experience running a couple of servers already, Taylor, Parker and I banded together to organize FLOOF! Our first meet was at the Skypad in the MSC. We ended up meeting a bunch of people. One of the people there was Bit, who would go on to make TFC. Floof in its early days didn't get funding so we had to pay for pizza and cookies, and we were in that one kind of small room in the second floor of the MSC. <br><br> Nowadays, I know whenever I go to a floof event, it's always going to be a place that is welcoming and accepting and fun, you know, above all else. I just think it's such a cool thing that from just a group of people who wanted to make friends, things could become as big as FLOOF which is so inspiring.
{: .small} 
<cite>— Stasi (Former Vice President)</cite>
{: .small}

Milestones: We broke 100 Bullsconnect members in 2024, 200 in 2025, and 300 in 2026.

### Stoney History

> When we had started up floof we wanted to make posters stickers etc for floof but we were very worried about using Rocky, USF's mascot, as USF can be picky about how and where he is used. In hindsight I dont think it would have been an issue, but we thought we could just circumvent that by making our own club mascot!
{: .small} 
<cite>— Taylor</cite>
{: .small}

<img src="\assets\images\history\stoneyconcept.png">{: .align-center style="width: 75%;"}
All art in this section by Alexandra!
{: .small .text-center}

> I remember we were very fond of Rocky and his already anthropomorphic presence around campus. Taylor, Stasi and I put our heads together and came up with a sweeter and fluffier mascot for the club. We decided on a highland bull as their golden coat fit well within USF's color palette! We decided to touch on some green ears and horns to make that theming more apparent. We teasingly came up with the name Stoney as it was synonymous to Rocky but quickly grew attached to the name and kept it on. 
{: .small} 
<cite>— Alexandra</cite>
{: .small}

<img src="\assets\images\history\rockystoney.png">{: .align-center style="width: 75%;"}

> I remember enthusiastic scribbling down concept sketches and personality poses of him and Rocky interacting. They started out as room mates and distant relatives but soon they became brothers! Competitive but supportive, Stoney takes on more alternative sports like skateboarding and surfing, and loves taking it slow by exploring local wildlife and tending to the plants at the USF botanical gardens.
{: .small} 
<cite>— Alexandra</cite>
